C-Section Recovery

Comprehensive in-home rehabilitation beginning as soon as you're discharged home. As Asheville's postpartum PT who comes to you, we provide the rehabilitation that the system doesn't automatically order — but every C-section mom deserves.

  • Scar tissue mobilization & desensitization
  • Core & pelvic floor reconnection

⚠ Post-Surgery Red Flag Screening Included
  • Fever, unusual redness or warmth at incision site
  • Increased swelling, discharge, or wound separation
  • Signs of blood clot (DVT) — calf pain, swelling, warmth
  • Shortness of breath or chest pain
  • Abnormal blood pressure or heart rate
  • Severe headache or vision changes

We monitor your vitals and screen for red flags every visit — and refer immediately when needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon after a C-section can I start in-home PT?

We can begin within days of your discharge home. As Asheville's postpartum PT who comes to you, early intervention — even gentle, appropriate movement — is one of the most important factors in a complete recovery.

Is this cash pay? Do you take insurance?

Resilient Bloom is a cash pay physical therapy practice in Asheville. You pay directly — no insurance delays, no authorizations. We provide superbills so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are accepted.

What does a typical first visit include?

Your first session is a full evaluation — vital signs, post-surgical red flag screening, movement assessment, pelvic floor and core function, and a full review of your symptoms. You leave with a personalized plan and tools to start healing immediately.

Can I be seen even if I had a vaginal birth?

Absolutely. All postpartum bodies benefit from pelvic floor and core rehabilitation. Tearing, episiotomy, prolonged pushing — all have specific recovery needs that in-home postpartum PT in Asheville addresses directly.

What areas of Asheville do you serve?

We provide mobile physical therapy throughout all of Asheville, NC and the surrounding area. During your free consultation we'll confirm your location is within our service area.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

North Carolina is a direct-access state — you do not need a physician referral to see a physical therapist. You can book cash pay PT in Asheville directly with Resilient Bloom at any time.
